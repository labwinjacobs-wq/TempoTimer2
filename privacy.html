Privacy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Without an account

Your task list and Friday checklist are kept in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

With an account

If you sign up, we store your email address, your tasks, and your completed focus sessions so they can appear on your other devices. Each account can only read its own rows.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is used only to identify you. It does not give Tempo access to your calendar, mail or anything else in your Google account.
