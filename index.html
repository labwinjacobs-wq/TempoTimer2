      3 / HOW TO USE IT

      The routine

      1. Dump everything

        Put every commitment in the list first, even the small ones. It's easier to cut than to remember.

      2. Mark three

        Set at most three tasks to High. If everything is high, nothing is.

      3. Add up the minutes

        The summary under "Your tasks" totals your estimates. If it's more than the hours you have, something has to move.

      4. Run the timer

        One task per session. Take a real break afterwards, away from the screen.

      4 / FRIDAY REVIEW

      Five minutes on Friday

      Look at what got done, what didn't, and why. Be honest, not harsh. The point is to make next week's list more realistic than this week's.
